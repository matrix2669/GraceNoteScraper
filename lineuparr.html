Google Maps opens only when you click the lookup button. Save & test sends the pasted address to the providers listed above. No Google API key is needed.

Show instructions and example
  1. Enter your street address and click Find in Google Maps. A new tab opens with your lineup ZIP included.
  2. Select the correct result. Check that the street, city, state and ZIP describe your service address.
  3. Copy the full address beside the map-pin icon in the place details, highlighted below. Use the copy-address control if available, or select and copy the text. Do not copy the search-box text, a place name, a Plus Code, or the Share link.
  4. Return here and paste that address, including apartment/unit details if needed. Keep the street spelling and directionals exactly as shown; Northeast is not always interchangeable with NE.
  5. Click Save & test. Review each provider's result, then click Scan providers in this ZIP below to collect enrichment. Saving does not start a scan.

Example only; use your own service address. Google Maps layouts may vary. A Google Maps address is not a USPS verification; only a successful provider lookup is marked provider-verified.
